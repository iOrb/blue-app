Blue 1.0 — notes, adaptable collections, and alarms.

Only notes and collections, with existing folder content preserved. Notes open in rendered Markdown and remain editable. Custom visual indicators work consistently in lists, item details, and filters: pale gray when off, colored when on. Favorites use a star.

Ask Blue to capture items, add or remove fields, change icons, edit notes, and fill basic public facts with Wikipedia source links. Enter sends; tapping outside leaves the chat; the microphone offers on-device dictation when available. Manual editing works independently of model availability.

Install from Xcode with your free Personal Team. Simulator inference can use the explicitly configured local Mac model bridge. Hardware model, microphone, signing, and alarm delivery still require an iPhone check. No TestFlight setup is required.
